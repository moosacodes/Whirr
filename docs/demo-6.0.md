# Demo 6.0

## Three-minute storyboard

### 0:00-0:20 - The ordinary object

Show an unmodified household fan. Say: "This machine has no payment system. I am going to teach software what counts as a completed job."

### 0:20-0:50 - Teach

Open `/studio/promises`. Record real idle and running takes from the microphone. Point out MIC CLOSED before recording and the real meter only while recording. Train the model. Show the READY or LIMITED gate and the confusion matrix. Say clearly that a synthetic demonstration is labelled synthetic.

### 0:50-1:15 - Promise

Enter: "Charge $2 for one complete cycle. The machine must run for at least 15 seconds and return to idle. Do not charge if it stops early or the microphone disconnects." Show the exact compiled terms, price ceiling, failure policy, model digest, and SHA-256 agreement digest. Publish only after owner approval.

### 1:15-1:45 - Failed attempt

Open the public promise and consent. Choose a demo execution or a Sandbox execution. Start the machine and stop it early. Show `INTERRUPTED`, no eligible capture, and the proof that explains the failed minimum. No PayPal capture is requested.

### 1:45-2:20 - Qualifying attempt

Create a new execution and authorize the fixed ceiling in PayPal Sandbox if configured. Run the machine through the verified minimum. Show the required stage advance, return to idle, and `QUALIFIED`. Press capture only after re-evaluation. Show the provider-confirmed result. Do not splice unrelated provider evidence.

If Sandbox credentials or a buyer are unavailable, stop at the qualification screen and say the provider action is OPEN. Do not stage a fake capture.

### 2:20-2:45 - Evidence

Open the portable proof. Show the exact terms hash, model hash, observation ledger, temporal checks, eligibility reason, and separate trust domains. Emphasize: "The proof records what was observed and what was inferred. It does not prove the microphone was honest or that a product was made correctly."

### 2:45-2:55 - The idea

"Whirr lets ordinary people define what physical work is worth and makes that promise executable with AI and PayPal."

## Judge Mode path with no physical object

1. Open `/judge` and note the Open gates.
2. Open `/promise` and the seeded reference agreement.
3. Read the synthetic provenance label and model limitations.
4. Consume the consent checkbox.
5. Choose demo observation and start the execution.
6. The microphone remains visibly closed unless the judge grants access and presses start.
7. Use the seeded replay executions or the documented seed for a resolved demo proof.
8. Open the proof verifier and inspect the recomputed checks.

The synthetic judge path demonstrates contract mechanics and the user experience. It does not simulate a provider capture.

## Physical operator checklist

- Use a safe, unmodified appliance.
- Fix microphone distance.
- Record at least two takes for each core class.
- Do not call synthetic takes a field trial.
- Test an interrupted attempt first.
- Use a fresh execution for a qualifying attempt.
- Never claim a capture without a PayPal Sandbox provider id.
- Keep `.env` and browser cookies out of the recording.
- Keep the final video under three minutes and upload publicly only when the human operator is ready.
